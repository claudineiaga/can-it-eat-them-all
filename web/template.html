Can It Eat Them All?

How this experiment works

A circular arena starts with 24 balls. A wall collision creates a new ball if that ball’s 3-second reproduction cooldown has expired. The balls pass through one another.

The eater follows the nearest ball at a constant speed with a fixed turning limit. Its area increases with every capture. The experiment ends when no balls remain or the population reaches 300.

This is a toy simulation with invented rules. The run is reproducible: restarting repeats the same conditions and result. The rules stay the same throughout.
